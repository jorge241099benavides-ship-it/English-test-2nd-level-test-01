Section I: Simple Past of "To Be" (was/were) — 30 Points

A. Multiple Choice: Choose the correct option. (5 points, 1 pt each)

1. I ___ at home yesterday.



2. She ___ very happy about the news.



3. ___ they in Granada last weekend?



4. We ___ tired after the game.



5. The movie ___ good.



B. Fill in the blanks with "was", "were", "wasn't", or "weren't". (5 points, 1 pt each)

1. Carlos at the park yesterday. (affirmative)

2. My parents at work; they stayed home. (negative)

3. you a good student in high school? (question)

4. It raining this morning. (affirmative)

5. The dogs hungry yet. (negative)

C. Sentence Correction: Find the mistake and write the correct sentence. (5 points, 1 pt each)

1. They was at the market.

2. Was you late for class?

3. I weren't sad.

4. We was good friends.

5. The weather were hot yesterday.

D. Unscramble the sentences. (5 points, 1 pt each)

1. was / yesterday / she / sick / .

2. at / were / they / school / ?

3. home / I / not / was / at / .

4. very / the / was / test / easy / .

5. tired / you / last / were / night / ?

E. Reading Comprehension (10 points, 2 pts each)

"Yesterday was a great day for Maria. She was in Masaya with her family. The weather was sunny and hot. Her brothers were very excited because they were at the artisan market. Her mother was happy, but her father was tired."

1. Where was Maria yesterday?

2. How was the weather?

3. Why were her brothers excited?

4. Was her mother tired?

5. Was it a bad day for Maria?

E. Reading Comprehension (10 points, 2 pts each)

"Last year, there was a big festival in León. There were many colorful dances and there was delicious food. There weren't any cars in the center of the city because there were too many people walking. At night, there was a beautiful firework show."

1. Where was the festival?

2. Were there cars in the center of the city?

3. Was there a firework show?

4. What kind of food was there?

5. Why weren't there any cars in the center?
